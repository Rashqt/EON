# Terms and Conditions

Last updated: 2025.

## What EON is

EON is an experimental, independently developed artificial-life
simulation software project. It generates a synthetic world and
simulated organisms according to rules defined in its source code, and
lets you observe, save, and inspect the results.

## What EON is not

- EON is not a scientifically accurate reconstruction of Earth's history,
  biology, or evolutionary record. Its organisms, traits, and mechanics
  are simplified and designed for an interesting, legible simulation,
  not for scientific accuracy.
- EON's simulated organisms are computational agents. They are not alive,
  sentient, or conscious in any sense, and nothing produced by this
  software should be interpreted as evidence of artificial consciousness
  or general intelligence.
- Results produced by EON (population counts, trait trends, species
  counts, extinction events, and similar output) are computational
  outputs of the specific rules implemented in this codebase. They should
  not be cited or relied on as biological, ecological, or scientific
  evidence about real-world evolution.

## Known limitations

This version of EON has real, documented limitations (see the
"Limitations" section of `README.md`), including a batch/CLI-only
interface rather than a live interactive graphical Observer Mode,
simplified single-cell predation-free ecology, and performance
constraints at very large population sizes. These limitations are
disclosed as part of using the software honestly; they are not hidden or
minimized elsewhere in the project's materials.
